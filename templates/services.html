QUESTIONS

Before you reach out.

How long does a typical project take?

A standard business website usually takes a few weeks. Web applications, e-commerce platforms and custom software take longer, depending on scope. Every quote includes a realistic timeline before work starts.

Do you only build for businesses in Nigeria?

No — Entrix II works remotely with clients anywhere. Projects like Jecyani Properties and Crownbee Global Services span real estate businesses across multiple regions.

Can you redesign my existing website instead of building new?

Yes. Many engagements start with a technical or SEO audit of an existing site, followed by a redesign or rebuild focused on the issues that are actually holding it back.

What's the difference between a website and a web application?

A website presents information — pages, content, contact forms. A web application does work — logins, dashboards, databases, payments and business logic. Most projects sit somewhere on that spectrum, and we scope accordingly.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Post-launch support, monitoring and ongoing feature work are available through a retainer, so the product keeps improving after it ships.
