OUR VISION

From digital solutions to World-built technology.

We should have companies building ambitious products for our realities, our creativity and our future. Entrix II's long-term vision is to grow from a digital engineering company into a technology company creating software, hardware and connected experiences.

That journey will take time. We are starting where we are strongest: building useful digital products, learning from real users and developing the engineering foundation required for much bigger ideas.

PROJECT: MIRROR X

The first name on the hardware roadmap.

Entrix Mirror X is the name we've chosen for our first planned device release. It represents the point where the Entrix II vision moves beyond websites and software into physical technology.

This is a future product concept, not a product currently available for sale. We want to earn the right to build it by first becoming excellent at engineering, product design, customer experience and reliable technology.
