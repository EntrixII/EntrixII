FAQ

Common questions from new agents.

How is the 30% commission calculated?

30% of the final project amount the client pays. If a project closes at ₦500,000, your commission is ₦150,000. No caps, no sliding scale.

When do I get paid?

Commission is paid once the client's payment has cleared and the project has started. Specific payout timing and method are agreed with each agent in writing before referral.

Do I need technical skills?

No. You only need to introduce businesses that need digital services. Entrix II handles scoping, proposals, development and delivery.

How long does approval take?

Most accounts are reviewed within 24–72 hours. You'll receive an email notification once your account is approved.

Can I submit leads I don't personally know?

Yes — as long as the lead is genuine, you're the one who made the introduction, and the business hasn't already been in contact with Entrix II. Duplicate or pre-existing leads cannot be claimed.

Is there any cost to join?

No. Registration and lead submission are completely free. You only earn — you never pay.
